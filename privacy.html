Data protection measures

We apply strong security measures to protect your personal data at all times. Access to information is strictly controlled and limited to authorized users only. We use secure systems to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or misuse of data. Regular security checks are performed to identify and fix potential risks. Our systems are continuously updated to meet modern security standards. We are committed to ensuring your data remains safe and confidential.

Anonymity guarantees

All reports are handled in a way that protects your identity. We do not collect unnecessary personal information that could identify you. Your submission remains anonymous throughout the entire process. Even during investigations, your identity is not disclosed. We take privacy seriously and prioritize confidentiality at all stages. You can report concerns with confidence and peace of mind.

Who has access to reports?

Only authorized and trained personnel can access submitted reports. Access is granted strictly for investigation and resolution purposes. Staff members follow confidentiality rules when handling reports. All access to data is logged and monitored for security. Unauthorized access is strictly prohibited and prevented by system controls. We ensure that your information is handled responsibly and securely.

Data retention policy

We retain personal data only for as long as it is necessary. The retention period depends on legal, operational, or reporting requirements. Once the data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized. We follow strict guidelines to ensure proper data lifecycle management. Regular reviews are conducted to determine what data should be kept or removed. This helps minimize risk and protect your privacy over time.

Encryption/security explanation

We use advanced encryption to protect data during transmission and storage. This ensures that your information cannot be read by unauthorized parties. Secure communication channels are used whenever data is transferred. Our systems follow industry-standard encryption protocols. Regular updates are applied to maintain strong security protection. These measures help ensure your data remains safe and confidential.
